ERP CSV Export

Entity-level validation. Any selected group with an invalid payment reference, missing Payment Method, Bank Charge, Bank Account or Value Date, or pending approval blocks that entity.

Why this tab exists — Citi only: this tab builds the CSV files (one per entity, 13-column template) that are uploaded into ERP when the payment run goes through Citi bank. Payments routed through another Payment Bank (HSBC / Standard Chartered / Deutsche Bank) do not need an ERP CSV from here — they follow that bank's own submission process, so rows whose Payment Bank is not Citi are not meant for this upload even when they appear in an entity's file. Processing Calendar & Settlement Forecast

Payment-run days (đi lệnh): every Wednesday, except a Wednesday falling inside a book-closing window — month-start (first 4 working days) or month-end (last day − 3 → last day). Expected Completion = Value Date + processing working days per currency.

Run-day rule: payments are submitted on Wednesdays only, except during the two book-closing windows: month-start close — the first 4 working days of the month (weekends don't consume the count) — and month-end close — from (last calendar day − 3) to month-end. A Wednesday inside either window is blocked; when a window misses Wednesday, that week runs normally. Working day = Mon–Fri; bank holidays follow the destination-calendar master below.
